# Tabs: social Home, Screen Time, Friends

Status: mostly decided (2026-11-21).

## Idea
Three tabs:
1. **Home** (social): your peers and their statuses, plus an activity feed of what happened with
   your peers and stat updates from friends.
2. **Screen Time**: today's breakdown sheet from Home, as its own tab, plus other stats.
3. **Friends**: as today.


## Decided
- 2026-11-21: **Feed has two layers.**
  - Peers: every event that is a push today (hit limit, asked for more time, locked, given extra
    time, Pressure on/off) appears for that person's peers, so a missed or failed push is still
    seen.
  - All friends (peers included): milestones, persona changes and social events (new peers or
    friends), to liven things up.
- 2026-11-21: **Read-only feed** for now. Emoji reactions are the likely next step.
- 2026-11-21: **No leaderboard for now; parked.** Home already holds the peers row and the feed, so
  there may be no room. Revisit alongside screen time totals.
- 2026-11-21: **Your own status moves to the Screen Time tab** (ring, time used vs limit, Pressure,
  lock or extra time). Home is only others. Your profile picture in Home's top-left corner gets the
  same status ring as peers, so your state is still visible at a glance.
- 2026-11-21: **Screen Time tab layout:** today's usage on top (ring, breakdown), your raw stats below
  with today / week / year / all time. Settings stays for settings.
