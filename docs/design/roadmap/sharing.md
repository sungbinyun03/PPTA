# Sharing and invite incentives

Status: invite link and peer-slot gate decided (2026-11-21); more unlocks later.

## Idea
Like Beli: make sharing easy, and gate some features behind inviting people, so users want to
bring friends.

## Decided
- 2026-11-21: **Sharing means an invite link**, purely to bring in new users. Opening it after sign-up
  lands the new user on a friend request to the inviter. No stat or persona cards for now.
- 2026-11-21: **Peer slots are gated:** 10 by default, 20 once you've brought in enough people (number
  to decide). Feed, personas and stats stay core and are never gated.

## Follow-up (after the invite link works): more unlocks to brainstorm
Ideas so far, none decided: cosmetics (ring styles, app icons, persona badge skins; overlaps with
IAP, earn or buy), a founding / Influencer badge for big inviters, early access to new features for
top inviters, and a future power feature such as weekday vs weekend limits.

## Open questions
- How many joined invites raise the peer cap to 20? Does a join count only once the invitee is active?
- Rewards for the person who invites (and the invited)?
